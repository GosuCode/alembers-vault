For my BCA 7th-semester internship I worked at **Golden Technosoft**, a software
company building web and mobile products. I joined the team behind
**BusineSSathi ERP**, a cloud ERP platform for inventory tracking, financial
management, and customer relationship handling.

## The organisation

Golden Technosoft builds web applications, business automation, and analytics
products, working across React, Flutter, Java, PHP/Laravel, Node.js, MySQL,
MongoDB, and WordPress. The internship placed me in the development unit
responsible for the ERP product.

## What it does — BusineSSathi ERP

- **Inventory** — stock tracking with automated restocking alerts.
- **Finance** — day-to-day financial management in one system.
- **Customers** — relationship handling and records in the same place.
- **Analytics** — real-time dashboards and graphs for business data.
- **Accessibility** — cloud-based, so it works from anywhere.

## What I worked on

- Built and implemented **backend features** with Node.js and MongoDB.
- Developed and optimised **frontend components** with React and Ant Design.
- Worked on **API development and integration** between the front end and back end.
- Built a feature to **upgrade users from the free tier to premium**, including the payment flow.
- Optimised **API responses** for faster dashboard graph loading.
- Used **Docker** for containerisation and a consistent local environment.

## Stack

| Layer         | Choice                                  |

| Frontend      | React, Ant Design                       |

## Notes and limitations

There is no repository — the code belongs to the company, not to me — but the
BusineSSathi ERP is live and linked above. Some advanced features were left out
