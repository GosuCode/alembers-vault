Artisan Nepal (built as **DOKO**) is my BCA 6th-semester project: an online
marketplace for handmade products and original artwork in Nepal — a dedicated
storefront for local artisans rather than a general-purpose shop.

## The problem

Artisans in Nepal often lack a direct route to buyers and depend on middlemen,
which eats into already-thin margins. DOKO gives them their own listings, order
and sales tooling, and direct contact with customers.

## What it does

- **Listings** — artisans add, edit, and manage products with images and details.
- **Discovery** — buyers browse and filter by category.
- **Reviews** — customers rate and review products.
- **Orders** — order tracking from placement through fulfilment.
- **Sales analytics** — dashboards so sellers can see how their shop performs.

## Stack

| Layer      | Choice                                  |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Frontend   | Next.js, React.js, Tailwind             |
| Backend    | Node.js, Express.js                     |
| Database   | PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM             |
| Auth       | JWT sessions                            |
| Media      | Cloudinary for product images           |
