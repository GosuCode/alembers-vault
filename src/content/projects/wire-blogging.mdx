WIRE is a full-stack blogging platform: writers create accounts, publish and
edit posts from a dashboard, and readers browse, search, and comment. It was my
4th-semester project, split into three repositories — a reading web app, a Node
API, and a small CMS.

## What it does

- **Accounts and sessions** — readers and authors log in to reach their dashboard.
- **Post management** — create, read, update, and delete posts from the CMS.
- **Comments** — readers leave feedback on posts.
- **Search** — find posts by keyword.

The abstract sums up the intent best:

> The system will allow users to create, read, update, and delete blog posts
> through a user-friendly web interface… providing bloggers with a reliable
> platform to share their content with the world.

## Stack

| Layer    | Choice                        |
| -------- | ----------------------------- |
| Frontend | React, Tailwind CSS           |
| Backend  | Node.js, Express.js           |
| Data     | MySQL, Sequelize ORM          |
| Hosting  | Vercel (frontend)             |

## Repositories

It is split across three repos: `wire` (the public reading app), `wire-api`
(the Node/Sequelize/MySQL backend), and `WIRED` (the authoring CMS).

## Known limitations

The demo link is a Vercel deployment from 2023; the repositories are archived and
no longer deployed or maintained, so parts of the demo may be offline.
